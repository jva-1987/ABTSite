Password recovery workflow (external users)

  1. Open the weblink https://sp.login.siemens.com/.
  2. Click Log in.
  3. Enter the email address and click Continue.
  5. Click Forgot password?.
  6. The email address of the user displays automatically in the Email address field.
  8. Click Continue.
  9. The Check your email window displays.
  11. Go to your email inbox and click Reset my password.
  12. An external link opens in a new tab.
  14. Enter the new password in the New password and Re-enter new password fields and click Reset password.
  1. The application changes the password and sends a notification.
