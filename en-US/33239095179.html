NOTICE

Do not use the IPv6 if BTL certification is required

The current implementation of BACnet/IPv6 in ABT 6.1 is not eligible for BTL certification. This is primarily because BBMD/FD (BACnet Broadcast Management Device/Foreign Device) functionality is not yet fully implemented, which is a prerequisite for BTL certification on the BACnet/IPv6 Data Link.
