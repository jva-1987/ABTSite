Export chart properties

The export chart data in the Object view > Chart objects extracts the list of charts & its properties.

  1. Open the chart objects list. See Show properties of a chart object.
  2. Select the chart from the Chart structure drop-down.
  3. Click .
  1. A .CSV file is generated with all chart and its properties.
