ADA_DLY: Adaption for anti-glare protection delay

Block ADA_DLY (FB805) corrects the delay time for frequently changing brightness, after anti-glare protection is cancelled.

Application: Centralized shading function AFN {CenFcd01}, CHT {CenBrgtShd02}

Functioning

Condition

Result:

Measured time < [PrlgFac] * [TiAda]

[TiAda] (new) = [TiAda] (old) + [CorrTiAda]

[PrlgFac] *[ TiAda] <= Measured time <= [ShrtFac] * [TiAda]

[TiAda] (new) = [TiAda] (old)

Measured time > [ShrtFac] * [TiAda]

[TiAda] (new) = [TiAda] (old) – [CorrTiAda]

[TiAda] is limited by [LgtSnsDlyMin] and [LgtSnsDlyMax].
