Field bus

Desigo devices support different field buses depending on the type used:

  • On-board I/O bus
  • TX-I/O bus
  • KNX PL-Link bus
  • M-bus
  • Modbus:
    • Modbus RTU network
    • Modbus TCP network

The corresponding properties can be displayed or, if required, operated for each bus.
