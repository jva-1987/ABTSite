Adapting the RDG2 central seasonal compensation

By adjusting the flow temperature to the outside temperature, a constant indoor climate is maintained. This leads to greater comfort for building users, as temperature fluctuations are minimized.

  1. The Central seasonal compensation is available.
  1. Go to Building > Building structure.
  2. Right-click a device and select Go to engineering.
  3. Open the KNX PL-Link task.
  4. In the KNX PL-Link area, select a device (room).
  5. Right-click and select Show in program.
  6. The Programming editor opens.
  7. In the Project tree, select [Room name] > Resources > KNX PL-Link > [Room name].
  8. Select the Device chart.
  9. Right-click and select Open.
  10. The device chart opens.
  11. In the Project tree, select [Central function] > BACnet > Central seasonal compensation.
  12. Drag the Compensated cooling setp.shift for comf. to the SpShftCCmfCmp FB.
  13. Drag the Compensated heating setp.shift for comf. to the SpShftHCmfCmp FB.
