QMX6.P34, P44, P74 template properties

The listed properties are displayed in the QMX templates editor > Inspector window > Properties tab when the templates is selected in the Project tree.

Marked properties ( 1) , 2) ) belong to a subsystem-specific extension. If the corresponding subsystem-specific extension is enabled, it overwrites the selected QMX6 template properties. As a precondition the property in the QMX6 template must be enabled.
