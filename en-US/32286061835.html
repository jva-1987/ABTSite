Restarting the MS/TP sub-device discovery

The MS/TP Restart sub-device discovery function is used to trigger the discovery or rediscovery of sub-devices that are part of a larger BACnet device. MS/TP sub-devices can be individual components, modules, or peripherals that are connected to and managed by the main BACnet device. As a result of the discovery process, the main BACnet device will enumerate the MS/TP sub-devices that are currently connected and available.

Function is not supported in this version and will display an error Value could not be changed.
